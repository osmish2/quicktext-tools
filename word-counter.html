About this tool

This word counter runs entirely in your browser — your text is never sent anywhere, so it's safe to paste drafts, essays, or anything private. It counts words by splitting on whitespace, characters both with and without spaces, sentences by terminal punctuation, and estimates reading time at 200 words per minute, the commonly used average adult reading speed.
